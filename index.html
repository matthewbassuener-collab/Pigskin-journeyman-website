ABOUT THE ROCKNE HOUSE

As featured on CBS's Staycation Sports, The Rockne House is more than a themed rental—it's a full-blown football experience, crafted for families, fans, and tradition-seekers alike. Located just one mile from Notre Dame Stadium on iconic Rockne Drive, this legendary stay sleeps up to 18 guests and was designed by Pigskin Journeyman to honor the heart of Fighting Irish football while delivering unforgettable comfort and fun for groups of all kinds.

Here's a fun fact for the diehards: The Rockne House sits at 2121 Rockne Drive—the same street number as the Pro Football Hall of Fame: 2121 George Halas Drive. Two football legends. Two iconic addresses. One legendary stay.

Whether you're here for a game, graduation, reunion, or summer getaway, The Rockne House is ready to host your crew in style. Enjoy a massive 10-person hot tub, a private Football Flicks cinema with 110" screen and surround sound, and a backyard built for play—with a fire pit, basketball hoop, and BBQ deck. Indoors, you'll find a game room with pool, Pop-a-Shot, Football Toss, and walls lined with nostalgic touches that make every guest feel like part of the team.

The home offers five spacious bedrooms with KING memory foam beds, comfortably sleeping up to 18 guests. A fully stocked kitchen makes group meals easy, and multiple flat-screen TVs throughout the house are loaded with NFL+ Premium, Max, Hulu, Disney+, and Prime—perfect for watch parties, movie nights, or just unwinding after the game.

Whether you're building memories with your kids or reliving your college days, The Rockne House is your home field advantage in the heart of Fighting Irish country.

ABOUT THE HALL OF FAME SNUBS HOUSE

Welcome to the ultimate football fan retreat! This isn't just any sports-themed stay—it's a tongue-in-cheek tribute to the legends who should be in the Hall of Fame... but aren't (yet).

🏆 NO OFFICIAL JACKET REQUIRED! The theme of this house is a PARODY, meaning it's all in good fun—not affiliated with the actual Hall of Fame. But hey, we think our honorees deserve the recognition!

🏈 What's Inside?

  • In-Ground Pool (April–Sept.) & Hot Tub – Cool off in the pool or soak in the hot tub after a long day of football debates.
  • Garage Game Room + Gym – Stay active (or just competitive) with arcade games, weights, and fitness equipment. Train like a legend or just talk about how you could've gone pro.
  • Massive Spa Bathroom – Unwind in a huge walk-in shower with front body jets, a handheld fixture, and a large overhead rain flow. Prefer a soak? There's also a standalone tub—plus a Bluetooth speaker fan to set the mood.
  • Hall of Fame Gallery Dining Room – Guests can add their own football jersey to our "snubbed" collection. Bonus: it doubles as a ping pong space!

  • Outdoor Fun – Fire up the grill, relax by the firepit, and enjoy the backyard with plenty of space for a championship-worthy hangout.

I BUILT THE STAYS I WANTED TO FIND ON THE ROAD.

I was a Wisconsin kid chasing an improbable quarterback dream. That road took me through high school ball, a post-grad year in Ohio, college, arena leagues, seasons overseas, and football stunt work in film and TV.

When the playing days ended, my wife Paulinha and I kept roaming. Our first Green Bay rental started as a way to share my football story. Then guests brought theirs. That changed everything. Each new house became a place where the game’s history, the fans, and the weekend itself all belong in the same room.

These homes are personal. I designed them for the way football people really travel: big groups, long stories, multiple games on, and no one ready for the night to end.

Matthew, Paulinha, Koa and Rio
